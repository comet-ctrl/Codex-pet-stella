# Stella reference brief

## Verified identity

Stella is the scientist lead from Studio Wrong's original animated series *Stella's Materialism*. Studio Wrong is a Tokyo indie animation studio producing original stories and characters. The references below are thumbnails delivered with Studio Wrong's own public X posts; they are used only as visual grounding for new fan art.

## Defining appearance

- Short, layered mint/seafoam hair with spiky outward tips and darker teal shading at the lower layers.
- Oversized round goggles resting on top of her head, with dark metal rims and green lenses. The goggles are not a visor over her eyes.
- Very large lime-green eyes with distinctive thick black circular graphic outlines and lashes.
- Pale warm skin with clearly visible freckles across the nose and cheeks.
- Thin mint eyebrows, small nose and mouth, and a youthful stylized 3D anime face.
- Narrow black choker.
- Oversized highly reflective silver lab/utility coat with a black inner layer.
- Glossy black mechanical-looking gloves.
- Compact scientist/engineer silhouette; curious, slightly tired, clever, and deadpan.

## Pet adaptation

Translate Stella into a cute compact full-body Codex pet while preserving the exact head silhouette, head-mounted goggles, lime ringed eyes, freckles, silver coat, black choker, and black gloves. For lower-body details that are not fully visible in the official thumbnails, use simple dark fitted trousers and compact black utility boots so the design remains subordinate to verified cues.

Keep the original series' polished Blender/3D-anime feeling, with simplified reflective materials that remain readable at 192x208. Do not introduce a cyan fashion bob, green face visor, cropped black jacket, exposed midriff, shorts, or beige boots; those belonged to the rejected inaccurate concept.
